# Press release

A news-wire style announcement for partnerships, integrations with major vendors, funding, hardware launches, and events.

## Skeleton

1. Subtitle: one sentence on what the integration or partnership enables
2. Dateline paragraph: *City, Country — Date — The Olares team announced…*
3. What Olares is and who uses it, in one paragraph, tied to this news
4. What the partner or product adds, technically and concretely
5. One or two real, approved quotes with name and title
6. About the partner
7. Availability with links
8. Boilerplate: *About Olares*
