# Launch overview

An announcement for one app or feature that explains what it is, why it exists, and how it fits Olares. It is short, third person, and confident. It is not a release roundup, a tutorial, or a feature-by-feature paraphrase of the docs.

## Use it for

- A system or flagship app that launches or returns, such as Wise, or a major redesign of Files or Vault
- A significant feature with its own user problem, such as app access in LarePass, Router and the Model Console, Engine Base, or Olares CLI with Agent Skills
- A third-party app or agent arriving in Market as a joint story ("what X and Olares built together")
- A new out-of-the-box scenario on Olares One

Do not use it for version roundups (release post), getting started (tutorial), benchmarks (review), or head-to-head comparisons ([comparison.md](comparison.md)).

## Skeleton

| Section | Job | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening, 1 paragraph | The everyday problem, then the launch | Name the problem concretely. Give version and Market availability once. |
| What it is, 1 paragraph | Formats, sources, what it keeps for you | Include one line on why running on Olares matters. |
| Image | The main view | |
| Idea or name, 1 paragraph | The stance, in a "we" sentence | Replaces an executive quote. |
| Example, 1 paragraph | One realistic reader scenario | Concrete and current. Do not narrate the screenshot. |
| Image | The example in the product | |
| How it fits into Olares (H2) | Confirmed integrations | LarePass, models and agents on the same device, Market |
| How people use it (H2) | Four bold-lead bullets | One sentence each. Capabilities, not steps. |
| Image | A detail such as notes | |
| What's next (H2) | One or two restrained sentences | No roadmap promises unless approved |
| CTA | Availability and a docs link | One sentence |

Target 450 to 650 words and three images.

## Sample: Wise (approved structure, November 2026)

> What people save online tends to scatter. An article ends up in bookmarks, a paper in a Downloads folder, a talk in a watch-later list, and a podcast episode in yet another app. Coming back to any of it starts with remembering where it went. With Olares 1.12.8, Wise is available in Olares Market as a personal library that keeps all of it in one place, on hardware you own.
>
> Wise holds articles, PDFs, ebooks, videos, and podcast episodes, along with your reading progress and notes for each one. It follows blogs, YouTube channels, and podcasts through RSS, so new posts and episodes arrive in a single feed. Because it runs on Olares, the library and everything you write in it stay on your own device.
>
> The name comes from what happens after saving. Keeping something takes a second. Understanding it usually means coming back, reading it alongside other sources, and seeing what you thought the first time. Most tools stop at the saving. We built Wise for the part that comes after, where information you return to becomes knowledge, and over time, judgment.
>
> Someone following how AI coding tools are changing software jobs, for example, might save a new article on developers who miss writing code by hand, tutorials on running coding agents locally, and a few videos of people trying it at home. Tagged together, they sit in one view and can be read side by side over a few weeks.
>
> **How Wise fits into Olares.** Wise installs from Olares Market like any other Olares app and works with the rest of the system. On a phone, it opens in LarePass, so an episode started on the commute continues from the same spot at a desk. The library also sits on the same machine as the models and agents you run on Olares, instead of in a third-party service.
>
> **How people use Wise**
> - **Follow sources in one place.** Blogs, YouTube channels, and podcasts arrive in Feeds through RSS. Only what you save moves into your library.
> - **Save from anywhere.** Keep a web page or an online PDF from its link, or upload files from your device.
> - **Group by question.** Tag related items and open the tag as a view. New items with the same tag join it automatically.
> - **Keep the thought with the source.** Notes stay attached to each item, and Wise reopens every article, PDF, or episode where you left off.
>
> **What's next.** At launch, Wise focuses on saving, following, and returning to what matters to you. We'll keep refining the experience and exploring better ways to help you manage what you know.
>
> Wise is available now in Olares Market for Olares 1.12.8 and later. To get started, read the Wise guide.

Metadata used: H1 *Wise: A Personal Library on Your Own Cloud*. SEO title *Wise on Olares: A Personal Library on Your Own Cloud*. Slug `olares-wise`. Focus keyphrase *Olares Wise*. Cover title *Wise* with the subtitle *For everything you meant to come back to*.

## Questions to settle before drafting

- What problem does a reader have before this app exists? Get it in one concrete sentence.
- Which formats, sources, and save paths are supported today?
- Which Olares integrations are confirmed: LarePass, Files, Router, models, agents?
- Is there a real, approved quote? If not, write the stance as a "we" sentence.
- Is any future direction approved for publication? If not, use the restrained line.
