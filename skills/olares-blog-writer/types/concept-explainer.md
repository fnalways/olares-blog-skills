# Concept explainer

Explains how one part of Olares works and why it is designed that way. These posts answer questions readers and AI search engines ask, so they suit GEO: give a clear definition early, describe each component's role, and say when the reader should care.

## Skeleton

1. The everyday experience that hides the mechanism, such as opening an app at one address
2. A plain definition of each part and the job it does
3. How the parts work together, with a diagram where useful
4. When this matters to the reader: a decision, a risk, a setting
5. Links to the docs for procedures
